# Sabrine Amri, Ph.D.

Postdoctoral Researcher in Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence at **Polytechnique Montréal**.
Ph.D. in Computer Science, Université de Montréal (2024).

## Research

I work on making AI systems trustworthy:

- **Privacy risks in LLMs and generative AI** — measuring and mitigating privacy leakage in LLM applications
- **Auditable AI agents** — neuro-symbolic and multi-agent systems whose decisions can be inspected
- **Content provenance** — governance-aware extensions of the C2PA standard
- **Disinformation detection** — explainable AI for fake news, misinformation and claim verification
